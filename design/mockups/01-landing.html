Testnet only

All balances, transactions and rewards are on Nimiq Testnet. Testnet NIM has no monetary value.

Operator treasury

Payouts are ordinary transfers from an operator-controlled treasury. This is funded and auditable, not trustless escrow.

Scripted requester

The agent is a scripted demo requester controlled by the operator, not a live AI agent. Evidence is bundled, not fetched live.

Session protocol / 03 steps

How the session runs

Terse. Server-authoritative.

Sign in with Nimiq Pay

One-time signature / HttpOnly session
